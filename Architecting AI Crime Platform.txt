### Planner Response

A **Data Pipeline** is simply a set of automated steps that takes raw data from a source, transforms it (cleans it, formats it), and loads it into a destination (like a database or a search index) so your application can use it. 

In the context of this hackathon, your team lead is going to generate raw mock data (likely as CSV or JSON files) using an LLM. **Your job (creating the data pipeline) is to write a script that takes those raw files and pushes them into your system.**

### What your Data Pipeline needs to do:
For your KSPD Crime Intelligence Platform, your data pipeline will have **three simple steps** (often called an ETL pipeline - Extract, Transform, Load):

1. **Extract**: Read the raw CSV/JSON files your friend generates.
2. **Transform**: 
   - Ensure the columns match our exact database schema (e.g., `CaseMasterID`, `CrimeNo`, `BriefFacts`).
   - Clean up any messy data the LLM might have hallucinated.
3. **Load**:
   - **Load to Relational DB**: Insert the records into your SQLite database (for local dev) or Zoho Catalyst Data Store (for production).
   - **Load to Vector DB**: Take the `BriefFacts` column from the FIRs, pass them through the embedding model, and save them into the FAISS index so the AI Copilot can search them.

We actually already built the foundation for this! 

1. **`backend/scripts/generate_mock_data.py`**: Right now, this script *invents* its own data and inserts it into the database. You just need to modify it slightly to *read from your friend's CSV files* instead of generating random data.
2. **`backend/scripts/build_embeddings.py`**: This is the second half of your pipeline. Once the data is in the database, this script automatically extracts it, transforms it into vector embeddings, and loads it into FAISS.
